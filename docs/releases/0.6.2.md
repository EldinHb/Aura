## What's new

### Android app: notifications and workspaces

- **Notifications.** The app keeps its connection to your computers open in the
  background and notifies you when a feature owner asks you a decision, when a main
  chat (not a worker) replies, and when an approval or question waits on you. Reply
  to a message, or Allow / Decline an approval, from the notification; tapping one
  opens the session. Turn it off in Settings → "Stay connected in background". Aura
  asks for notification permission after your first pairing.
- **Workspaces from the phone.** Create and rename workspaces, move a session (with
  its workers) to another workspace, and pick a workspace when starting a session.
  Paired devices may now call `workspace.create`, `workspace.rename` and
  `session.set_workspace`; deleting workspaces stays desktop-only. Update the desktop
  to 0.6.2 for this.

## Fixes

- Decision cards on Android are readable on every wallpaper colour, light and dark:
  they sit on plain surfaces and the Answer button is no longer faded.
- Choosing a workspace for a new session still works when the project or agent list
  fails to load.
