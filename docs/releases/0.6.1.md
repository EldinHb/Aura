## What's new

### Android app: sessions the way the desktop shows them

- **Grouped session list.** Sessions are grouped by workspace, and within each
  workspace into Needs you, Working and Idle, in the desktop sidebar's order. Groups
  fold, and the app remembers what you folded per server.
- **Scheduled tab.** Sessions started by scheduled tasks have a tab of their own,
  one section per task, newest run first.
- **Workers and teams inside their session.** An orchestrator's workers and a feature
  owner's team no longer show up as separate entries. Their leader's row counts them,
  and the session screen has a tab per worker, each with its own transcript,
  composer, approvals, interrupt and stop.
- **Decision cards in the chat.** Questions a feature owner puts to you show in its
  chat where they were asked, and you answer them there (the inbox uses the same card).

## Fixes

- A session opens at its latest messages instead of the first one, follows new
  messages while you are at the bottom, and shows a "jump to latest" button with a
  count of new entries when you scroll up.
- Aura's own messages (worker reports, decisions, teammate questions) no longer look
  as if you wrote them; they show as a collapsed "from Aura" row.
- Typing a message capitalizes sentences and uses autocorrect.
- The newest message no longer overlaps the one above it while it streams in.
