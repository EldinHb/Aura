## What's new

### Usage and cost on the desktop

- **What every turn spent.** Under each reply, one line with its tokens (input,
  output, cache), how full the context was and the cost; estimates from list prices
  are marked with `~`. Hover or click for the detail.
- **Dashboards.** A session's own dashboard (with its workers, for orchestrators and
  feature owners), and a global one: a period against the one before, cost or tokens
  per day by model or agent, and the biggest sessions, features, projects, models
  and agents. `⌘K` → Usage and cost.
- **Summaries.** A written summary of today, yesterday, this week or any period, to
  read and copy as Markdown, and if you like one every day, with a notification
  (Settings → Usage). Usage is kept by the daemon, so it survives restarts.

### Session templates

- Save the new-session dialog's options (agent, model, effort, modes, folder,
  worktree and branch pattern, workspace, group, prompts, autonomy) as a template and
  fill the dialog from it next time. Rename and delete them in Settings → Templates.
  Templates are shared with paired phones.

### The desktop as a remote client

- **Other Aura computers in one window.** Pair with Aura on another machine (a home
  server, another Mac) from the new computer switcher at the top of the sidebar, and
  switch between them (`leader h h`). Sessions, transcripts, approvals, new sessions,
  usage and templates follow the computer shown; settings, mods and scheduled tasks
  stay this computer's.
- **Safe by design.** The remote's certificate is pinned when you pair and a
  different one is never accepted silently; device tokens live in the system keychain
  (Keychain, Credential Manager, Secret Service). The inbox and the badge count every
  connected computer, and what a remote does not allow is hidden or says so.

### Android app

- **Images.** Attach photos and screenshots to a message, or send them from any app
  with **Share to Aura**. Images in the transcript open full screen.
- **Changes.** A session's diff on the phone, for every session and worker tab:
  the files the agent changed with a numbered unified diff each, read-only and limited
  to the session's own folder. Tap a line and **Ask for changes** puts its `file:line`
  in the composer.
- **Widget and Quick Settings tile.** The widget shows how many items need you across
  your servers and the first few of them; tap one to open its session. The tile shows
  the count and opens the inbox.
- **Usage and templates.** The usage dashboards, the per-turn usage line and the daily
  summary notification; start a new session from a template.
- **Notification settings.** Turn Decisions, Messages, Needs approval and Scheduled
  tasks (off by default) notifications on or off.

Update the desktop to 0.6.4 for the phone's Changes, usage and templates.

## Fixes

- A streaming reply no longer makes the Android transcript jump, and a code block
  still arriving is not shown as a stray backtick.
- Far fewer notifications on Android: a main chat's reply notifies only when it
  answers your own message.
- Git reads from paired devices are sandboxed: they never run anything the
  repository configures (hooks, filters, diff drivers, fsmonitor), stay within the
  session's folder, never follow symlinks out of it, and are bounded in size and time.
- Images attached on the phone are bounded in what they may cost to read and send
  (at most 10 per message, within the remote listener's message limit), so a huge or
  odd image cannot stall the app or be refused by the server.
